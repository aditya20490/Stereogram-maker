How to see 3D

  1. Rotate your phone to landscape.
  2. Look far away, like at a mountain peak. Don't focus on the screen.
  3. Hold the phone in front of you and slowly shift your focus closer to the screen.
  4. At one point it clicks and the picture pops into 3D.

Camera 3D tips

  1. Pick a point and keep it in the green circle.
  2. Take shot 1, then slide the phone 5-8 cm sideways.
  3. Match the faded ghost image and don't tilt or move up/down.
